
= Reserved Terminology

The following terms are reserved for use within ODISC 
documentation and specifications.

These terms are defined to provide consistent meaning across 
all ODISC documents, ensure broad applicability, and align 
communication between all participants, manufacturers, 
developers, and governing bodies.

Terms listed in this section shall be used when referring 
to ODISC concepts unless a defined exception applies.

Vehicle::

The term "Vehicle" shall be used instead of "Car" or 
"Slot Car" when referring to an ODISC-compatible racing unit.

Exception::

The terms "Car" or "Slot Car" may be used when specifically referring to legacy equipment, historical systems, or non-ODISC systems where the original terminology is relevant.
