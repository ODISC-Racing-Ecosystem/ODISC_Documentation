

= Appendix


== Introduction

This appendix provides additional information and resources related to the 
ODISC Racing Ecosystem. It includes a glossary of terms, document standards, 
and licensing information to support stakeholders in understanding and 
implementing the ODISC framework effectively.

NOTE: While this appendix does not maintain a revision number or history, 
documentation within the appendix may track such information as appropriate. 
Stakeholders are encouraged to refer to the individual documents for the 
most up-to-date information.
