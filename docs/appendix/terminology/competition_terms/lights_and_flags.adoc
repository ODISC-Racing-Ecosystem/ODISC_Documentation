= {doctitle}

The following standard colors are used for lighting within 
the ODISC Ecosystem. Each color has a specific meaning and 
is used to communicate the status of a race or event.

The shown examples of lights on the various displays are 
shown as representative of the pattern, not to indicate exact light placement.

== White

Uses: +
* General Lighting: Look how pretty! +
* Racing Conditions: Race is underway.

=== Race is underway

For general lighting, white is used to indicate that the race is underway. This is indicated by all lights being fully on and white without any other color visible.

=== White Flag

A white flag indicates that there is one lap left in the current heat or race.

Indication::
This event covers the full racetrack and is indicated by white lights without 
any other color visible. Every other light should be alternating on and off 
to create an increased flashing effect.

== Green

Uses: +
* General Lighting: N/A +
* Racing conditions: Race has started or resumed.

=== Green Flag

A green flag is issued to indicate that the race has started or resumed.

Indication::
The green flag is indicated by all lights being fully on and green without any 
other color visible. +
After a green indication for a number of seconds (configurable), indicators 
change to white or extinguish as appropriate.

== Yellow

Uses: +
* General Lighting: N/A +
* Racing Conditions: Yellow and Double Yellow Flags +

=== Yellow Flag

A single yellow caution is used when a car has stopped moving or another 
hazard exists on the track. When triggered, the system evaluates the incoming 
data and flags a section of the track, including approach areas, as being 
"under caution" and applies any specific race rules to it.

Indication::
The racetrack displays solid yellow on all lights without any other color visible, 
alternating with lights being fully off to create a fast flashing indicator.

=== Double Yellow Flag indication

If more than one yellow flag caution event is triggered, even if within the same 
section (such as two cars derailing together), a full track caution is triggered. 
This works similar to the partial caution, but applies to the entire track.

Indication::
The racetrack displays solid yellow on every other light without any other color visible. 
The lights alternate on and off to create an increased flashing effect (configurable timing).

== Red

Uses: +
* General Lighting: N/A +
* Racing conditions: Red Flag, session stopped.

=== Red Flag

A red flag indicates an unsafe condition, and as such, racing may not continue. 
Racing stops immediately. This is handled by shutting down all data transmission 
to the track and removing power from the rails. 

Indication::
Full red without any other color visible alternating in the same manner as double yellow lights, 
alternating with fully off to create a slow flashing indicator (configurable timing).

== Purple (Replaces Black)

Uses: +
* General Lighting: N/A +
* Racing conditions: Session finished or a Black Flag has been issued.

NOTE: As black is not a visible color, ODISC uses purple to indicate black when using lighting systems.

=== Black Flag

A warning, penalty, or disqualification has been issued for a vehicle.

Indication::
This is the only event that is not shown on track, but is instead indicated on the 
driver station for the appropriate vehicle, and may be indicated on the controller 
of the driver as well. +
Advanced vehicle decoders may also indicate this on the vehicle itself.
