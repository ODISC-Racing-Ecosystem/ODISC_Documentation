= Vehicle Type Requirements

ODISC categorizes vehicles based on both historical theme and physical 
architecture. When establishing a sanctioned racing class, the organizing 
authority must explicitly define the Vehicle Type boundary using the following 
four structural pillars.

NOTE: To enable common categorization of vehicles, any racing done at the 
Tier 3 (District) and above must be conducted on approved and published vehicle 
types included in the ODISC Racing Regulations. Organizers may submit a 
proposed vehicle type to the ODISC Racing Committee for review to be included 
in the ODISC Racing Regulations.

== Structural Pillars of Vehicle Type

=== 1. Body Style Configuration

Classes must be designated as either Open Wheel or Closed Wheel. 
Mixed-style grids are strictly prohibited to prevent catastrophic 
wheel-interlock accidents.

Open Wheel::
Vehicles have exposed tires with independent axle structures.

Closed Wheel::
Vehicles have integrated fenders encompassing the entirety of the 
running gear.

=== 2. Dimensional Boundaries

The type definition must enforce hard maximums and minimums for track dimensions:

* Maximum Track Width
** The absolute outer distance between the left and right tyre walls.
** Measured across the widest axle on a standard tech inspection block.

WARNING: See DS-001-100 for absolute maximums allowed for each scale.

* Minimum Ride Height
** The clearance distance between the flat track surface and the chassis underbelly.
** Measured at the lowest mechanical point under the car while stationary.

=== 3. Body Material Composition

Regulations must dictate the acceptable manufacturing medium of the shell:

* Hardbody
** Constructed from rigid injection-molded ABS plastic or heavy resin.
** Prioritizes scale weight, visual realism, and high crash durability.

* Performance Polyurethane
** Constructed from ultra-thin, highly flexible plastic variants.
** Engineered specifically to reduce top-heavy weight and minimize body roll.

* Vacuum-Formed Lexan
** Constructed from paper-thin, transparent polycarbonate sheets.
** Designed exclusively for low weight reduction classes and raw speed.

=== 4. Historical / Aesthetic Era

The category must specify a chronological or series-specific boundary to 
maintain visual harmony, scale realism, and thematic integrity across the 
starting grid.

Example Eras:
* 1960-1975 Vintage Formula 1
* Modern GT3
* 1970s Le Mans

NOTE: To maintain a functional competitive ladder, a sanctioned event at a
tier higher than Tier 4 (Club) cannot utilize a vehicle type unless that 
same vehicle type is actively utilized in sanctioned competition at the 
tier immediately below it. Higher-tier utilization does not mandate that 
any individual club or district must run the class, but it guarantees that 
a promotional pathway exists from the grassroots level upward.
