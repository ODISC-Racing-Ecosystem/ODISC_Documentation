Club racing is the foundational tier of organized ODISC competition. It 
establishes a structured environment where competitors can systematically 
develop driving skills, master official race procedures, and participate in 
sanctioned events.

Seasonal championship scoring is based on the final finishing position of each 
sanctioned Club competition. Competition points are calculated utilizing the 
ODISC Weighted Scoring Model, where total available points are dynamically 
scaled based on the number of active participants in the event.

[NOTE]
====
Each individual club retains complete autonomy over its competitive calendar. 
Clubs may schedule and implement their championship seasons on any timeline 
that suits their local membership, completely independent of higher-tier 
competition schedules.
====

Competitors may be promoted from the Club tier when they demonstrate consistent 
performance and technical proficiency against the local field. Promotion 
decisions are governed by the local organizing body or Member Organization 
based on a multi-race matrix rather than a single event outcome.

Official promotion criteria include:

* Consistent podium finishes across a single competitive season.
* Winning or placing highly in a sanctioned Club championship.
* Demonstrated mastery of ODISC track procedures, marshalling duties, and 
sportsmanship.
* Formal promotion recommendation from the host club's race director.

Competitors may remain at the Club tier indefinitely to continue developing 
their skills, support local club growth, or if higher-tier regional competition 
is geographically unavailable.

== Competitive Pyramid

As part of the ODISC competitive pyramid, the Club tier acts as the receiving 
ground for competitors relegated (demoted) from Tier 3 (District) at the 
conclusion of a season. 

Relegated drivers compete for full championship points at the Club level. 
However, active higher-tier competitors (Tier 3 or above) who choose to 
participate in a local Club event outside of relegation may only do so as 
non-scoring exhibition entries to protect the competitive integrity of the 
tier.
