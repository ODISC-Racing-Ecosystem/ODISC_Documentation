= Tier 5: Fun Race

Tier 5 races are informal events intended exclusively for 
entertainment, technical experimentation, and casual or 
recreational racing.

No championship points are awarded for Tier 5 races. Results 
may be recorded locally for personal tracking, but they do not 
contribute toward official ODISC rankings, player classifications,
or seasonal championship standings.

Tier 5 events enforce no eligibility, licensing, or skill-based 
entry requirements. Any competitor may participate regardless of 
previous racing experience, mechanical expertise, or official rank.

The primary purpose of Tier 5 is to provide a low-barrier entry 
point for new competitors to experience ODISC racing in a welcoming 
environment free from competitive pressure.
