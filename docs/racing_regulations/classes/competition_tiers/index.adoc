= Competition Tiers

ODISC implements a tiered pyramid system of racing, descending from elite 
national competition at Tier 1 down to casual recreational events at Tier 5.

Each tier increases in technical restrictions, sporting complexity, and driver 
eligibility requirements as competitors ascend toward the pinnacle of the pyramid.

[cols="1,2,7",options="header"]
|===

|Tier |Name |Purpose

|Invite
|International Invitational
|Elite invitational competition. +
The highest tier of global ODISC racing where top national champions compete.

|Tier 1
|Nationals
|Highest regular championship racing. +
Represents the top level of ongoing competition within a member nation.

|Tier 2
|Regional
|Advanced multi-district competition. +
Brings together elite competitors from multiple local districts.

|Tier 3
|District
|Local competitive development. +
Organized competition bridging multiple clubs within a geographic boundary.

|Tier 4
|Club
|Foundational organized racing. +
Structured local competition where procedures are learned and points are tracked.

|Tier 5
|Fun Race
|Introduction and enjoyment. +
Informal, open-door racing focused on recreation and social participation.
|===
