= Tier 2: Regional

Regional racing brings together the strongest competitors from multiple District 
boundaries, representing an advanced echelon of organized multi-club 
competition. 

Seasonal championship scoring is based on the final results of sanctioned 
Regional competitions. Points are awarded from final event standings utilizing 
the ODISC Weighted Scoring Model.

Competitors may be promoted from the Regional tier to the Tier 1 (Nationals) 
level when they demonstrate elite driving mastery and consistent success across 
the broader regional field. Promotion decisions are subject to final 
verification by the national organizing authority.

Official promotion criteria include:
* Winning or placing highly in a sanctioned Regional championship.
* Achieving multiple podium finishes at the Regional level within a single season.
* Demonstrated compliance with all national licensing and technical requirements.
* Formal promotion approval from the governing national authority.

== Grid Capacity and Entry Limits

Unlike Tier 4 (Club) and Tier 3 (District) levels—which permit unlimited driver 
registrations to foster grassroots growth—Tier 2 (Regional) and above introduce 
strict caps on grid capacities. 

The maximum number of active driver slots allocated to a Regional championship 
is determined explicitly by the regional organizing authority based on venue 
logistics, pit space, and race-day time constraints. Consequently, access to 
the Regional grid is strictly restricted to drivers who earn entry via formal 
promotion, ranking, or approved invitation.

== Competitive Pyramid

As part of the ODISC competitive pyramid, Tier 2 maintains a strict 
promotion and relegation interface with Tier 1 (Nationals) and Tier 3 (District).

Unlike lower tiers, Tier 2 carries no automatic performance-based protection 
safeguards from single-season declines. Drivers must maintain performance or 
face automatic relegation to Tier 3 (District) at the end of the season.

Competitors who finish within the designated promotion zone of the Regional 
championship standings earn promotion to Tier 1 (Nationals) for the subsequent 
season. Any competitor granted an official promotion retains the right of 
refusal and may choose to turn down the advancement to remain in their current 
tier without penalty.

Competitors who no longer wish to compete at the Regional level may request an
automatic demotion to the Tier 3 (District) level, thereby making room for more 
promotions of qualified drivers.

=== Field Stabilization

To stabilize grid capacities and balance the ladder, voluntary self-demotions 
directly alter the relegation and promotion mechanics on a strict 2:1 ratio. 
For every two (2) approved voluntary self-demotions from Tier 2:

* One (1) additional promotion vacancy is created, allowing an extra qualified 
driver from Tier 3 (District) to advance, provided they accept the promotion.
** For an odd number of voluntary approvals, this option is offered first.
* One (1) automatic, performance-based relegation is canceled.
