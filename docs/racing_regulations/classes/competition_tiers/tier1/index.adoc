= Tier 1: Nationals

Nationals racing represents the pinnacle tier of ongoing seasonal ODISC 
competition within a recognized member organization. It brings together the 
elite domestic field to compete for the ultimate title at the highest standard 
of sporting and technical compliance.

Seasonal championship scoring is based on the final results of sanctioned National 
competitions. Competitors earn seasonal points from their final event finishing 
positions utilizing the ODISC Weighted Scoring Model.

As the peak tier of the regular competitive ladder, there is no automatic 
seasonal promotion out of Tier 1. Instead, top-performing Nationals drivers 
earn eligibility to represent their member organization at the International 
Invitational (Invite Only) showcase events when they occur.

Official international eligibility criteria include:

* Winning or placing highly in a sanctioned Tier 1 National championship season.
* Demonstrated mastery across highly diverse track surfaces, types, and venues.
* Full compliance with all international licensing, passport, and technical codes.
* Selection or qualification through an officially approved national team process.

== Competitive Pyramid

As part of the ODISC competitive pyramid, Tier 1 operates as the definitive ceiling 
of the ongoing domestic ladder, maintaining a direct interface with Tier 2 
(Regional).

In accordance with grid capacity caps established for Tier 2 and above, Tier 1 
requires drivers to maintain active elite placement. Underperforming drivers 
finishing at the bottom of the active season standings face automatic relegation 
to Tier 2 (Regional) at the conclusion of the season.

Competitors who win or finish within the designated elite pool of the National 
championship standings earn invitation eligibility to the International 
Invitational (Invite) tier for upcoming events. Any competitor granted an 
international invitation retains the right of refusal to remain in Tier 1 
without penalty.

Competitors who no longer wish to maintain the rigorous travel or technical 
demands of the Nationals level may request an automatic demotion to the Tier 2 
(Regional) level, thereby opening up regular grid slots for upcoming drivers.

=== Field Stabilization

To stabilize grid capacities and balance the peak domestic ladder, voluntary 
self-demotions directly alter the relegation and promotion mechanics on a 
strict 2:1 ratio. For every two (2) approved voluntary self-demotions from Tier 1:

* One (1) additional promotion vacancy is created, allowing an extra qualified 
driver from Tier 2 (Regional) to advance, provided they accept the promotion.
** For an odd number of voluntary approvals, this option is offered first.
* One (1) automatic, performance-based relegation to Tier 2 is canceled.
