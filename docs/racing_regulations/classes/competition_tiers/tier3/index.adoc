District racing represents organized competition between multiple clubs within a 
defined geographic boundary. It introduces a broader competitive field where 
drivers must adapt to unfamiliar track layouts and diverse racing environments.

Seasonal championship scoring is based on the final results of sanctioned 
District competitions. Competition points are awarded using the ODISC Weighted 
Scoring Model, with points dynamically weighted according to the total number 
of unique competitors entered in the event.

Competitors may be promoted from the District tier when they demonstrate 
sustained performance against this broader field. Promotion decisions are 
subject to final verification by the regional organizing authority.

Official promotion criteria include:
* Multiple podium or top-tier finishes across sanctioned District events.
* Winning or placing highly in a sanctioned District championship.
* Demonstrated ability to compete successfully outside of their home club track.
* Formal promotion approval from the governing regional authority.

== Competitive Pyramid

As part of the ODISC competitive pyramid, Tier 3 operates a merit-based 
promotion and relegation relationship with both Tier 4 (Club) and Tier 2 
(Regional).

Competitors who win or finish highly within the designated promotion zone of 
the District championship standings earn promotion to Tier 2 (Regional) for the 
subsequent season. 

Conversely, automatic relegation to Tier 4 (Club) applies to competitors who 
finish at the bottom of the active season standings, or who no longer meet 
the minimum seasonal participation requirements defined by the regional 
organizing authority.

Competitors shall not face automatic relegation solely due to a single 
difficult or unfortunate racing season, provided they maintain active 
participation and adhere to regional sporting codes.

Competitors who no longer wish to compete at the District level may request an 
automatic demotion to the Tier 4 (Club) level, thereby making room for more 
promotions of qualified drivers.
