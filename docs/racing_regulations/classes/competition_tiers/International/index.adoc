= Tier: International Invitationals

International Invitationals represent prestige, non-pyramid showcase events 
bringing together the absolute top-ranked competitors from multiple sovereign 
national member organizations. Due to the logistical constraints of long-distance 
travel, these events are organized as standalone tournaments rather than ongoing 
seasonal championship schedules.

No global championship league points are tracked across Invite-tier events, and 
they exist completely independent of the domestic promotion and relegation ladder. 

Participation in an Invitational event is strictly restrictive and accessible by 
invitation only. Drivers cannot qualify directly via points accumulation within this tier; 
instead, grid allocation is determined by peak performance metrics achieved at the 
Tier 1 (Nationals) level.

Official invitational criteria include:
* Winning or placing as a top-ranked finalist in a sanctioned Tier 1 National 
championship season.
* Formal selection or endorsement by the driver's respective national governing 
authority.
* Full compliance with international technical specifications, hardware sizing, 
and digital signaling standards defined for the host venue.

== Competitive Pyramid

As a standalone prestige classification sitting at the apex of the ODISC framework, 
the International tier does not participate in the regular promotion or relegation matrix. 

Drivers who participate in an International Invitational return to their 
rightful domestic tier (typically Tier 1) at the conclusion of the event. 

Poor performance or failure to attend an invitation carries zero negative 
relegation penalties toward a driver's domestic standing, ensuring that elite 
racers can accept or decline international invitations without sporting risk.
