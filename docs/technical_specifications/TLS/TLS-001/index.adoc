== Introduction

[quote]
____
Without a track, there is no racing.
____

This document defines the common physical characteristics
of an ODISC racetrack. Specialized racetrack components
that require more detailed specification are defined in
their own TLS documents.

== Philosophy

Standardization of racetrack dimensions, interfaces, and
installation practices enables interchangeable hardware,
predictable vehicle behavior, and consistent competition
across the ODISC Racing Ecosystem.

== Capabilities

While a racetrack incorporates many standardized components, the
following are fundamental features of the racetrack itself.

=== Slot

This is the slot that the guide pin of a vehicle uses to constrain its
trajectory.

The slot shall be manufactured with sufficient depth and clearance to
prevent the guide pin from contacting sensors or other infrastructure
installed within the slot.

=== Power Rails

The power rails provide both electrical power and the communication medium
used to transmit commands to vehicles.

Power rails may use either copper tape or wire braid. If wire braid is used,
the required cutouts must be incorporated during the manufacturing process.

=== Common Mounting Points

A finished racetrack should provide common mounting points for various types of
hardware. This includes pockets and cutouts on the underside to simplify wiring
and installation of physical track infrastructure.

Mounting provisions should not interfere with normal operation when left unused.

NOTE: While the need for common mounting points is mentioned here, the actual
requirements are specified in the TLS and DS documents for each component.

=== Other Components as Designed

Additional components may be incorporated into a racetrack according
to the designer's intended use. Requirements for these components are
defined in their respective TLS documents rather than this document.
