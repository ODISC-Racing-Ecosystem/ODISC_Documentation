== Introduction

[quote]
____
Shared hardware allows reduced costs.
____

Without drivers, only autonomous vehicles would race.

Shared driver station hardware reduces cost while allowing each driver 
to bring their preferred controller and personal driving configuration.

A driver station is more than a controller interface. It provides the 
processing required to interpret controller input, apply permitted 
driver-specific configuration, communicate with the Race Management 
System (xref:../TLS-002/index.adoc#tls-002[TLS-002]), and present race information to the driver.

== Philosophy

The Driver Station provides a standardized interface between the driver
and the Race Management System.

Driver preference is separated from physical controller hardware.
Controllers provide input, while the Driver Station applies permitted
configuration, validates the resulting commands, and communicates with
the Race Management System.

This separation allows drivers to use different controllers while
maintaining fair competition through centralized enforcement of race
rules and Parc Fermé restrictions.

== Capabilities
The following items need to be provided by each driver station.

=== Driver Identification

The Driver Station shall support identification of the current driver.

NOTE: The method for this is TBD.

=== Controller Connections

Each driver station must provide connections where a driver plugs in their 
controller. A driver station may provide a second set of connections to 
enable driver swapping mid-race, such as for endurance racing, or to 
connect a backup controller in case of control issues.

=== Controller Calibration

Variations in controller hardware shall not provide a competitive 
advantage. The Driver Station shall support controller calibration 
by determining the minimum and maximum throttle positions and mapping 
them to the standardized operating range.

Calibration may also verify that supported controller inputs operate correctly.

=== Handheld Controller

The Driver Station shall provide a common interface for communication 
with compliant handheld controllers. The interface shall include a 
method to validate controller capabilities to ensure compliance with 
ODISC racing regulations.

The compliant controller interface intentionally limits available controller
features. This ensures that differences in controller hardware do not create
an unintended competitive advantage and allows low-cost controllers to remain
competitive with higher-cost alternatives.

==== Compliant Handheld Controller

A compliant controller shall provide the following inputs:

* Throttle Control
** No curve adjustments may be made by the controller
* Lane Selection
** Must support Left, Straight, and Right indications

A compliant controller may provide the following additional inputs:

* Brake Initiation (on/off)
* Vehicle Effect Controls

A compliant controller may provide the following feedback features:

* Race Status Lights
* Rumble Feedback

Feedback features shall only present information received from the 
Driver Station through the defined interface and shall not generate 
independent race information nor maintain internal counters.

WARNING: Any features not listed in this specification shall not be present
to comply with Parc Fermé rules during racing.

==== Vehicle Effects

Vehicle Effects may be used for non-competitive vehicle
functions such as:

* Horn activation
* High beam activation
* Turn Signals
** Tied to lane selection actions
* Other approved vehicle accessory functions

Available Vehicle Effects and communication requirements are defined by
the applicable ODISC Data Specifications.

Any Vehicle Effects shall not affect vehicle performance, 
race outcome, or provide competitive advantage.

=== Driver Configuration

The driver station provides the ability to adjust throttle curves based 
upon driver preference to move away from a purely linear throttle curve. 
Any such curves are applied prior to sending the throttle request to the 
Race Management System.

Throttle curve adjustments may be locked out due to Parc Fermé rules.

=== Display and Feedback

The Driver Station shall provide feedback allowing the driver to verify 
identity, configuration, and race-related information appropriate to 
the current session.

=== Race Management Communication

The Driver Station communicates with the Race Management System using
the ODISC Communications specifications.

The Driver Station shall transmit validated driver inputs and receive
race information required to provide driver feedback and enforce race
rules.

=== Input Validation

The Driver Station shall validate received controller inputs before
transmitting commands to the Race Management System.

Invalid, unsupported, or out-of-range inputs shall be rejected or handled
according to the ODISC Communications specification.
