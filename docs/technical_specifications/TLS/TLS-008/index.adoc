== Introduction

[quote]
____
Before a driver can race, the vehicle and driver must first be introduced.
____

The Docking Station provides the interface used to associate a Vehicle
Decoder (xref:../TLS-010/index.adoc#tls-010[TLS-010]) with a Driver Station
(xref:../TLS-009/index.adoc#tls-009[TLS-009]).

A vehicle needs to be paired with a Driver Station before it can
participate in a race. Otherwise, the Race Management System
(xref:../TLS-002/index.adoc#tls-002[TLS-002])
does not transmit vehicle control commands to the decoder for safety
reasons.

A Docking Station may be dedicated to a single Driver Station or shared
between multiple Driver Stations.

== Philosophy

The Docking Station provides a controlled environment for validation,
firmware management, and pairing.

* Validation ensures that the Vehicle Decoder is compatible with the
current ODISC racetrack.
* Firmware management updates the Vehicle Decoder to the current ODISC
firmware as required.
* Pairing establishes the communication relationship between a Driver
Station and a Vehicle Decoder.

The Docking Station does not participate in race operation.

== Capabilities

The Docking Station provides the following functional capabilities.

=== Driver Station Association

The Docking Station shall support identification of the Driver Station
to be associated with a Vehicle Decoder.

The method used to establish the Driver Station association is
implementation defined.

=== Decoder Validation

The Docking Station shall validate the connected Vehicle Decoder to
ensure compatibility with the current ODISC racetrack firmware and
ODISC communication protocols.

The Docking Station shall provide validation status to the user through
the available status indicators.

=== Firmware Management

The Docking Station shall support firmware updates for connected Vehicle
Decoders.

=== Vehicle Pairing

The Docking Station shall provide the ability to pair a Vehicle Decoder
with a Driver Station.

During the pairing process, the Vehicle Decoder shall receive the Driver
Station identifier used for race communication.

=== Status Indication

The Docking Station shall provide status indication for pairing,
validation, and firmware operations.

Status indication shall be provided through:

* Indicator lights or display on the Docking Station.
* Indicator lights on the Vehicle Decoder.
* Indicator lights or display on the Driver Station.

NOTE: Status indication from all involved components provides confirmation
that the pairing process completed successfully and that communication
between the Driver Station, Docking Station, and Vehicle Decoder is
operational.
