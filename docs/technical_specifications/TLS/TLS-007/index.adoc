== Introduction

[quote]
____
If you can't see, how do you know what happened?

Light it up!
____

Track lighting provides visual communication between the race system,
drivers, and spectators. It improves visibility of the racetrack while
providing a standardized method to communicate race conditions and events.

== Philosophy

ODISC uses lighting as a primary method of communicating race conditions.
The implementation of lighting may vary between venues, but the meaning
of each indication shall remain consistent.

A venue may implement lighting through full track illumination, dedicated
race condition indicator panels, or a combination of both.

== Capabilities

The Track Lighting System consists of one or more lighting systems that work
together to provide visibility and race status information.

=== General Track Lighting

General track lighting provides illumination of the racing surface and
surrounding areas.

The default state of general track lighting is white unless overridden by
a race condition indication.

=== Race Condition Indicators

The Track Lighting System shall support visual indication of race conditions
received from the Race Management System (xref:../TLS-002/index.adoc#tls-002[TLS-002]).

Race conditions may apply to the entire racetrack or to a defined section
of the racetrack.

Example: +
Sector B = Yellow Flag

The Track Lighting System shall not determine race conditions. It shall only
present the current state received from the Race Management System.

A venue may implement race condition indicators using:

* Dedicated light panels positioned around the racetrack.
* Changes to the color or behavior of general track lighting.
* A combination of both methods.

The displayed meaning of each lighting state shall conform to the ODISC
Flag and Light Color definitions.

NOTE: Refer to "Flag and Light Colors" in the appendices for the definition
of colors and patterns associated with race events.
