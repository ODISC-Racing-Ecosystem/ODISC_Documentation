== Introduction

[quote]
____
The vehicle is the visible part of racing.

The decoder is what enables the vehicle to execute ODISC commands.
____

The Vehicle Decoder provides the interface between an ODISC vehicle and the
Race Management System (xref:TLS-002/index.adoc#tls-002[TLS-002]).

The decoder receives commands from the Race Management System, controls vehicle
behavior, and provides the interfaces required for vehicle identification
and detection on the racetrack.

The mechanical design of the vehicle is outside the scope of this
specification. This specification defines the requirements for the decoder
and its integration into an ODISC-compatible vehicle.


== Philosophy

The Vehicle Decoder translates race control logic into vehicle behavior.

Vehicle behavior shall be determined by commands received from the Race
Management System and shall not be caused by independent vehicle logic.

This separation ensures that different vehicle platforms can participate in
ODISC racing while maintaining consistent competition rules and behavior.

== Capabilities

The Vehicle Decoder provides the following functional capabilities.

=== Race System Communication

The Vehicle Decoder shall communicate with the Race Management System using
the ODISC Communications specifications.

The decoder shall receive commands required to control vehicle operation,
including:

* Throttle control
* Brake control
* Race Status Codes

The decoder shall not independently determine race conditions or apply
race rules.

=== Decoder Identification

The Vehicle Decoder shall provide a unique identifier allowing the Race
Management System to distinguish individual decoders during communication
and race operation.

=== Motor Control

The Vehicle Decoder shall provide a mechanism for controlling the vehicle
motor.

The decoder shall translate received vehicle commands into motor control
signals according to the defined ODISC operating specifications.

The motor interface shall prevent commanded vehicle behavior outside of the
commands received from the Race Management System.

=== Detection Transmitter Interface

The Vehicle Decoder shall provide an interface for vehicle detection on
ODISC racetracks.

The detection transmitter shall transmit according to the applicable 
detection protocol.

==== Detection Transmitter Installation

ODISC utilizes a center slot placement for the vehicle detection
transmitter.

This chosen placement allows reuse of prior investments made when
purchasing slot cars and provides compatibility with existing vehicle
platforms, including:

* Slot.it oXigen (Type B & C Universal): 13.5 mm from the guide pivot center.
* Scalextric Digital (DPR Chassis Plate): 16.5 mm to 17.5 mm from the guide pivot center.
** This distance varies slightly by vehicle model to provide clearance for
front axle assemblies.

ODISC recommends minimizing the distance between the guide pivot and the
detection transmitter.

A shorter distance reduces the risk of detection loss when a vehicle is
fishtailing or operating at extreme steering angles.

=== Lighting

The decoder shall provide the ability to control at least four (4) lights
on independent channels.

Lighting channels may be used for normal vehicle lighting functions or
race status indication.

When used for race status indication, lighting behavior shall operate
according to race status codes received from the Race Management System.

=== Vehicle Effects

The Vehicle Decoder shall support execution of Vehicle Effect commands
received through the ODISC Communications specifications.

Vehicle Effects shall only be executed when commanded by the Race
Management System.

The Vehicle Decoder shall not independently generate Vehicle Effects.

=== Power Interface

The Vehicle Decoder shall provide the required interfaces for receiving
vehicle power and supplying connected components.

The decoder shall manage power delivery to ensure connected components
operate within their defined electrical requirements.

=== Firmware and Configuration

The Vehicle Decoder shall support firmware updates using the
ODISC Docking Station (xref:TLS-008/index.adoc#tls-008[TLS-008]).

Firmware versions shall be identifiable to allow verification of
compatibility with the current ODISC racetrack firmware and communication
protocols.
