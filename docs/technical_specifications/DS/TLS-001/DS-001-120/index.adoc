== Introduction

This DS defines the physical, electrical, and functional requirements for the 
standardized Power Tap Assembly module. This module mounts underneath an ODISC 
track bed to cleanly interface standard power supply wiring harnesses to the 
track surface power rails defined in xref:../DS-001-110/index.adoc[DS-001-110].

== Physical Requirements

=== Mounting Footprint Envelope

The power tap assembly shall attach securely to the standardized power tap 
mount on the underside of the track.

Dimensions::
The physical implementation profile envelope of the assembly board should not 
exceed 50 mm x 20 mm.

Hole Pattern Array::
The assembly shall feature 4x mounting screw clearance drop holes. The hole 
centers must be configured to a strict 40 mm x 10 mm +/-0.1mm pattern layout, 
supporting M3 mounting hardware with a hole diameter profile of 
Ø 3 mm +/-0.1 mm.

=== Component Interface

Power Connection::
The power tap assembly shall provide two independent input connection segments 
for each electrical pole to facilitate clean serial loop-through (daisy 
chaining) of distribution lines under the track layout. 

Rail Mounting Points::
The top-side board interface shall include mounting points dedicated to 
securing and conducting power directly to the track surface rails (tape or 
braid conductors) passing through the track drop hole cutout.

NOTE: The method of these mounting points (solder, screw terminal, etc.) is 
explicitly not specified in this standard. Examples may show options, but 
these shall not be considered part of the specification.

== Functional Requirements

=== Power Throughput

Power tap assemblies shall support continuous, stable power delivery operation 
at 24 V DC and 20 A.

Transient Hardening::
Power tap assemblies shall be designed with sufficient thermal and copper mass 
to withstand power transients and temporary short-circuits that occur during 
normal racing operations.

=== Integrated Signal Termination

To ensure uniform signal impedance and suppress data packet reflections back 
into the branching network hubs, high-frequency line termination is managed 
locally at each power tap site.

Termination Components::
Every power tap module *shall* feature an onboard passive termination resistor 
network (See example assembly, item R1 below) bridged directly across the 
isolated positive and negative power tracks. This automatically absorbs data 
signal echoes without requiring track builders to configure external discrete 
termination components.
