

= Power Tap Assembly Mount Dimensions and Sample Implementation

This diagram shows how a power tap assembly could be implemented with 
two screw terminals for each pole to allow easy daisy chaining of 
multiple power taps.

The rendered power tap also has solder pads for attaching the power rail 
(tape or braid) to the power tap mount.
